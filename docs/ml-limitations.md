# Machine-learning limitations

## Valid research question

Are newly received observables anomalous relative to Betelgeuse's historical distribution, and which physical/evolutionary scenario is more compatible with the total received data under stated assumptions?

## Invalid output

The project forbids an image classifier or unsupported probability that Betelgeuse "has already exploded." No ground-truth label exists for the inaccessible remote simultaneous state, and an algorithm cannot defeat causal information limits.

## Baselines first

Neural models must improve on a relevant transparent baseline: Lomb-Scargle, Gaussian process, robust MAD score, PCA/SVD, change-point analysis, autoregression, or the simple continuum spectral-index fit.

## Leakage controls

- Temporal hold-out rather than random neighbouring samples.
- Whole-epoch hold-out for images and spectral cubes.
- Instrument hold-out to expose metadata/resolution shortcuts.
- Simulation-family hold-out to test robustness to physical assumptions.
- Beam matching and astrometric registration before 2015/2023 imaging comparisons.

## Required outputs

Reconstruction or predictive uncertainty, calibration/coverage, false-positive rate, OOD behaviour, ablations, and a model card. Acceptable language includes "outside the historical training distribution" and "more compatible with scenario A under the stated assumptions."

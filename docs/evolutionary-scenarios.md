# Evolutionary scenarios

## Scenario A - core helium burning

Assumptions:

- The roughly 400/416-day signal is the radial fundamental mode.
- The roughly 2100/2170-day variation is a Long Secondary Period with a plausible companion contribution.
- Stellar models compatible with Joyce et al. place Betelgeuse in core helium burning.

The companion interpretation has independent theoretical, radial-velocity/astrometric, direct-imaging, and radio-atmosphere evidence ([Joyce et al. 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.09837), [Goldberg et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.09089), [MacLeod et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.11332), [ESO programme 114.28H9 release](https://archive.eso.org/cms/eso-archive-news/release-of-sphere-data-betelgeuse.html)).

Conclusion language: "Under the core-helium-burning interpretation, collapse is not imminent on human timescales." Do not turn an uncertain long stellar timescale into an exact countdown.

## Scenario B - late core carbon burning

Assumptions:

- The roughly 2200-day variation is the radial fundamental mode.
- The roughly 420-, 230-, and 185-day variations are overtones.
- Matching models are in late core-carbon burning.

Saio et al. obtain models in which carbon exhaustion can occur on a less-than-about-300-year scale, followed by shorter advanced-burning stages. This is a conditional model result, not a direct observation ([Saio et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.00287)).

Molnár, Joyce, and Leung argue that the stellar radius required by this interpretation conflicts with angular-diameter constraints ([Molnár et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05600)). The companion supplies an alternative physical origin for the LSP, weakening the need to treat it as a radial fundamental mode; it does not algebraically disprove every carbon-burning model.

## UI contract

Every scenario result must display its assumptions, source family, observational compatibility, and uncertainty. Switching an assumption recomputes the conclusion; no default is hidden behind an unexplained prediction label.

Betelgeuse historical photometry, generated by science/ingestion/aavso_history.py
AAVSO AID records used: 51460 (48378 Vis.; 3082 V).
Overview: separate 90-day medians and quartiles for Vis. and Johnson V.
Recent detail: separate 30-day medians and quartiles from 2010 onward.
Herschel: 27 visual magnitudes reconstructed in Lloyd (2020), Table 1.
No interpolation is inserted across missing intervals. Magnitude decreases upward in plots.
AAVSO acknowledgement: We gratefully acknowledge the contributions of the AAVSO observer community, whose photometric data and metadata resources were used in this study and made available through the AAVSO's scientific archives.
